FAQ

OEM / ODM questions

What is the MOQ for Robot Vacuum OEM/ODM orders?

Stock models start from 1 pc; OEM terms typically start at 1 pcs, with volume tiers from 2 pcs.

What is the difference between OEM and ODM?

OEM builds on our existing models using your brand and packaging. ODM starts from our platform and changes appearance or function to create a model exclusive to you.

What can be customised?

Logo, colour and packaging (Color Box / Corrugated Box / Gift Box). Product certifications on file: per model.

Can I order a sample first?

Yes. Sample quantity 1 pcs, sample lead on request; mass production on request.
