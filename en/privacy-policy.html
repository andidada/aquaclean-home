Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how AquaClean, operated by Hong Kong Damaijia Technology Co., Limited, handles information you provide through this website. It is a general notice; specific data-processing terms may be set out in a separate written agreement.

Information We Collect

We collect the details you submit in enquiry or contact forms, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, country and message, together with any product or certification documents you choose to send us.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to answer enquiries, prepare quotations and samples, process orders and after-sales requests, and send the materials you ask for.

We do not sell your information. We share it only when necessary to fulfil an order, for example with logistics or inspection providers, or when required by law, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.
