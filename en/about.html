Our Story

Built on a Simple Mission

Founded in 2015 in Ningbo, Zhejiang, AquaClean began with one goal: make professional-grade cleaning technology accessible to households everywhere. What started as a single handheld vacuum line has grown into a full portfolio of smart cleaning appliances.

Today, our 10,000 m² factory combines in-house R&D, automated assembly, and a strict three-stage quality system — delivering consistent, certifiable products at scale for partners worldwide.

  • 10,000 m² owned manufacturing facility in Ningbo
  • 60+ engineers in R&D, firmware, and industrial design
  • 3-stage quality control: IQC → IPQC → OQC
  • 50+ countries served, 200+ active B2B partners
