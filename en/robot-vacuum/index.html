Buying Guide

How to Choose a Robot Vacuum Cleaner

Five things to compare before you place a wholesale order.

01

Suction Power

Current range: 5000Pa · 3500PA · 1000–1499 Pa · 20000Pa. Higher Pa lifts more debris; compare Pa together with motor type (brushed vs brushless).

02

Navigation

Current range: LDS SLAM · LDS+SLAM · LDS Navigation. Laser/SLAM mapping builds a real floor plan; gyroscope units wander.

03

Runtime

Current range: 120–150 min · 150-180mins · 120 mins. Runtime decides how much floor one charge covers — check whether the battery is removable.

04

Battery capacity

Current range: 5200mAh. Battery capacity in mAh is the main driver of runtime; removable packs extend service life.

05

dust box capacity (l)

Current range: > 2 L · < 0.5 L · 0.6-1 L · 2 L. Dust box and water tank size decide mopping area per cycle.
